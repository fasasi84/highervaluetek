# Queen SusaStrategy Limited

Queen SusaStrategy is building an AI-powered business-insights product for Nigerian small-business owners and managers. The MVP focuses on analyzing authorized sales, customer-feedback and operations data to surface trends and themes for human review.

The product is at MVP stage. This repository contains the company website, not the MVP application. Website visuals are illustrative and are not live product screenshots. The company website does not provide product access, accounts or a data upload portal.

## Run locally

No build step or package install is required. Open `index.html` for the company site or `data-services.html` for the AI business-insights MVP overview. The legacy `demo.html` URL redirects to the product overview.

## Product overview

- Target users: Nigerian small-business owners and managers.
- Potential data: authorized sales, customer-feedback and operations records.
- Analysis focus: business data analysis and AI-assisted interpretation of trends and customer-feedback themes.
- Intended output: insights for human review alongside business context.
- Confirm supported data, product capabilities and handling requirements with the company before use.
- Infrastructure, pricing and market demand continue to be refined.

## Infrastructure and data boundary

This repository contains a static HTML, CSS and JavaScript company website, not the MVP application. The website has no accounts, hosted contact form, client-data upload portal or AI integration. Cloud and GPU infrastructure are still being evaluated; no provider or accelerated-computing stack has been confirmed here. Do not send confidential business data by email.

The About, Privacy and Terms pages should be reviewed against the company's actual legal and hosting arrangements before publication.
