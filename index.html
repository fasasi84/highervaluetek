AI & machine learning · our starting point

Apply AI where it can serve a clear business strategy.

AI and machine learning are the technology foundation for our startup's direction. Our current MVP is the first workflow, not an AI product: we are establishing the problem and data foundation before model development or performance claims.

Business strategy first

Identify a specific business decision or workflow where better information could make a practical difference.

AI/ML feasibility

Determine whether the use case, available data and operating context justify machine-learning or other AI approaches.

Current MVP boundary

The planner organizes business inputs and applies a transparent readiness rubric. It does not call, train or run a live AI or machine-learning model.

Responsible development

Future outputs should be evidence-linked, evaluated with users and subject to human review; they should not replace professional judgment.

Our strategy-to-MVP approach

Start with the business need, then prove the workflow.

Understand the business

Define the goal, the decision to improve, the people involved and the constraints that matter.

Map the information

Review relevant processes, authorized data and readiness, as captured in the current MVP.

Test a practical direction

Use a focused business assessment to examine fit; assess AI/ML only where it addresses a validated need.

Evaluate and improve

Agree on measures, learn from users and refine the strategy before expanding scope or technology.

AI/ML technology direction

Choose tools to serve the strategy, not the other way around.

Cloud, data and accelerated-computing options are future evaluation choices, not deployed infrastructure. Any architecture should follow the business use case, data safeguards and measured workload.

Business data and governance

The planner runs locally in the browser and does not send assessment data to an AI service. Future AI work would first need an authorized use case, clear data ownership, privacy and security requirements, representative test data, and human evaluation criteria.

Business problem definitionData readinessPrivacy and accessHuman evaluation

Model evaluation, when justified

Generative AI, machine learning and conventional automation are possible future approaches, not current product capabilities. Select a method only after the business case, data, risks and expected value have been tested; keep evaluation proportionate to the use case.

Current product status

Business AI opportunity planner at MVP stage

The planner captures a business challenge and readiness factors, produces a scored opportunity brief, recommends a first use-case direction, and can save or export assessments locally. It uses a transparent rules-based rubric, not live AI/ML. Hosted workspaces and verified business pilots are not yet available.

Next validation: learn from business users, confirm the problem and data requirements, and agree on useful measures before developing model-based AI capabilities.

Why this direction

Strategy and technology work best when they start with the same problem.

Businesses have growing volumes of operational information, but value depends on connecting it to real decisions. We are starting with business strategy and a focused MVP, then will evaluate where AI and machine learning can responsibly support better-informed work.
