About Our Company

Higher Value Teknical Services Ltd is a professional engineering maintenance and construction company delivering reliable technical solutions to industrial facilities, oil & gas companies, and infrastructure projects. Our company focuses on quality service delivery, safety compliance, and modern engineering practices.

With experienced engineers, technicians, and project managers, we support mechanical works, shutdown maintenance, fabrication, installation, and general construction projects across Nigeria.
