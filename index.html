The problem

Small businesses often have data, but lack a clear way to use it.

Nigerian small-business owners and managers may have sales records, customer comments and operational information spread across separate tools or files. That makes recurring service issues, changing demand and costly process delays harder to spot early. The scale of this problem varies by business and needs validation with users.

01 / SALES

Spot changing demand

Compare sales over time and highlight shifts that a business owner may want to investigate.

02 / CUSTOMERS

Understand repeated feedback

Organize customer comments into themes so recurring concerns or requests are easier to review.

03 / OPERATIONS

Find process signals

Explore agreed operational records for recurring delays or exceptions that may need attention.

These are product focus areas, not claims of measured outcomes. Specific supported data and feature availability should be confirmed for the current MVP.

Why now

More business activity creates more information—and more to miss.

As small businesses manage customer interactions, sales and operations across digital channels, useful signals can be buried in everyday records. A focused analytics product could help owners review those signals without relying only on manual, disconnected checks.

Product hypothesis

Make routine business data easier to review.

The proposed product will test whether AI-assisted summaries and pattern detection can help owners prioritize questions and make better-informed decisions. The problem, workflow and value still need validation with prospective users.

Technology & Infrastructure

Build the platform architecture around validated needs and data safeguards.

No product cloud or GPU infrastructure is currently deployed. During product validation, the team intends to evaluate cloud services for protected data storage, application and API hosting, user access, analytics and monitoring; providers and architecture have not been selected.

Cloud infrastructure · under evaluation

A future hosted product may need controlled data storage, authenticated API access, tenant separation, audit logging, monitoring and deletion controls. Cloud hosting would support secure access and scale as demand grows; no provider or deployment is yet confirmed.
