AI-powered business and financial intelligence for SMEs.

Queen SusaStrategy is developing a centralized workspace to help small and growing businesses make smarter, faster, and more informed financial and operational decisions. The product brings financial performance, revenue growth, costs, cash flow, workforce expenses, and reporting into one accessible view.

Current product status

A functional sample workspace, entering market validation.

The MVP lets users explore a financial performance dashboard, review a business snapshot, test hypothetical scenarios, and export reports. The team is refining core functionality and user experience while engaging SMEs through pilot discussions. The workspace currently uses illustrative data; it is not a hosted commercial service.
