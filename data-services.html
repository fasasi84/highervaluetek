AI business insights · MVP stage

Help small businesses find useful signals in everyday data.

Queen SusaStrategy is building an AI-powered business-insights MVP for Nigerian small-business owners and managers. The product focuses on analyzing authorized sales, customer-feedback and operations data to surface trends and themes for people to review before deciding what to do.

01 / CONNECT

Review approved business data

The MVP focuses on selected sales, customer-feedback or operations information. Ask the team about supported data sources and access.

02 / ANALYZE

Surface patterns and themes

The product uses data analysis and AI-assisted interpretation to help users review trends and recurring feedback.

03 / REVIEW

Keep people in control

Present candidate insights with source context for a business owner or manager to check.

MVP use case

Investigate a change in sales or recurring service delays.

An MVP user can compare sales periods or review service records and customer comments to see what changed and which questions deserve follow-up. Specific supported data and results depend on the current product features and context.
