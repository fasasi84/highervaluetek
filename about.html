AI-led business strategy · applied AI & machine learning

AI at the centre of strategies that solve business problems.

Queen SusaStrategy Limited is a Nigerian company building an AI-led approach to help businesses find practical solutions across customer service, sales, finance, operations and decision-making. We start with the challenge and desired outcome, then shape a strategy around AI/ML, data, people and responsible execution. Our current MVP, the AI Opportunity Planner, is the first step in this direction—not a live AI solution.

Product status: The local-first AI Opportunity Planner captures a business challenge, goals, data and readiness factors, then generates a structured brief using a transparent rules-based rubric. It does not run a live AI model or verify business claims. Hosted workspaces and validated pilots are not yet available.

Our strategy

AI-led problem-solving across the business

We aim to help teams identify high-value challenges, explore AI-enabled options and move toward practical solutions with clear measures. Each strategy should consider business goals, workflow, data, people, risks and accountable human oversight. Engagement scope and outcomes must be agreed with each business.

Our first product

AI Opportunity Planner MVP

The local-first planner helps teams structure a business challenge, consider possible use cases and record readiness factors. Its output uses a transparent rules-based rubric; it does not yet include live AI analysis, and it does not solve every business problem. Use cases and data readiness need validation.

Who we aim to help

Businesses seeking AI-led solutions

Our intended audience is business owners, managers and teams looking for better ways to address challenges in service, growth, operations and decision-making. The first MVP helps teams explore where AI might fit, what business information is available and what should be validated before investment.

Technology direction

Responsible AI/ML feasibility

Future work may evaluate AI and machine-learning tools for specific, authorized business use cases. Model selection, hosting and integrations will depend on customer needs, data rights, security requirements and measured feasibility. No cloud or AI infrastructure is currently deployed for the MVP.
