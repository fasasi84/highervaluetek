Business strategy · AI & machine learning

Helping businesses move from strategy to action.

Queen SusaStrategy Limited is a Nigerian company focused on helping businesses clarify challenges, strengthen decisions and translate strategy into practical work. AI and machine learning are the technology starting point for our startup. Our current MVP, the AI Opportunity Planner, helps a business structure an AI-use-case assessment and identify practical next steps.

Product status: The local-first AI Opportunity Planner captures a business challenge, goals, data and readiness factors, then generates a structured brief using a transparent rules-based rubric. It does not run a live AI model or verify business claims. Hosted workspaces and validated pilots are not yet available.

Business strategy

Clarity, priorities and execution

Our strategy focus is to understand business goals and constraints, identify meaningful decision or process challenges, and shape practical next steps. Engineering and operational experience inform our initial areas of work. Engagement scope and outcomes must be agreed with each business.

Startup direction

Applied AI & machine learning

AI and machine learning are the starting point for the company's technology-led startup direction. The AI Opportunity Planner is a working, local-first business-assessment MVP that helps teams frame possible use cases and readiness. It does not yet include live AI analysis; use cases and data readiness need validation.

Who we aim to help

Businesses seeking practical strategy

Our focus is business owners, managers and teams looking to clarify priorities and make better-informed decisions. The first MVP helps teams explore where AI might fit, what business information is available and what should be validated before investment.

Technology direction

Responsible AI/ML feasibility

Future work may evaluate AI and machine-learning tools for specific, authorized business use cases. Model selection, hosting and integrations will depend on customer needs, data rights, security requirements and measured feasibility. No cloud or AI infrastructure is currently deployed for the MVP.
