Queen SusaStrategy · Nigeria-first

AI-powered business and financial intelligence for SMEs.

Queen SusaStrategy is developing a centralized workspace to help small and growing businesses in Nigeria make smarter, faster, and more informed financial and operational decisions. Owners can bring performance, cash flow, growth questions, and workforce expenses into one clearer view, without needing a dedicated finance team to get started.

Current stage: Functional sample-workspace MVP and market validation. Users can review financial snapshots, test hypothetical scenarios, and export reports in CSV or print-ready PDF. Subscription billing, automated payments, and full commercial onboarding are planned for later development; they are not live today.

MVP workspace

Know what is happening in the business.

Explore monthly revenue, operating costs, profitability, cash flow, business growth, and workforce expenses in one sample dashboard. Try what-if scenarios to see how changed assumptions affect the outlook, then export a business performance report.

AI and decision support

Make analysis easier to review.

The current sample uses a local linear-regression trend for entered revenue history and deterministic business rules for recommendations. AI-assisted analysis, richer forecasting, and scenario guidance are planned; outputs should be checked against business context before decisions.

SME subscriptions

Monthly SaaS plans for growing teams.

The intended model offers monthly subscriptions sized to SME needs, with accessible financial visibility and business reporting. Pricing, hosted accounts, and subscription billing are not yet available.

Enterprise packages

More context for larger organizations.

Enterprise packages are planned for larger businesses that need enhanced reporting, team-level financial intelligence, and additional analytics tailored to their organization.

Target market

For Nigerian SMEs without dedicated finance teams.

Start with small and growing businesses that need accessible tools for financial analysis, operational visibility, and strategic decisions without a sophisticated finance department.

Market entry

Begin in Abuja, then grow across Nigeria.

The company plans to validate the product through pilot discussions in Abuja, expand across Nigeria, and later serve international organizations seeking consolidated business intelligence and team-level reporting.

Planned infrastructure · subject to credit approval

Use Azure credits to build the cloud foundation.

If awarded, Microsoft Azure startup credits would support development and deployment of the hosted SaaS product, its data protections, and future AI capabilities. The services below are planned; they are not currently deployed.

Cloud, data, and access

  • Scalable hosting for reliable customer access.
  • Secure databases and storage for financial, operational, and reporting data.
  • Authentication, role-based access, and organization-level permissions.

AI and financial decision support

  • Planned Azure OpenAI integration for business insights, trend analysis, and recommendations.
  • Predictive revenue, expense, cash-flow, and profitability forecasting.
  • AI-assisted what-if simulations to compare decisions and financial outcomes.

Long-term vision

Make confident, data-informed growth more accessible.

Queen SusaStrategy aims to become a trusted business and financial intelligence platform for SMEs and growing organizations across Africa and beyond, combining financial visibility, predictive analytics, and useful AI-powered recommendations in one accessible workspace.
