Company and product

Engineering experience, applied to industrial reliability.

Queen SusaStrategy Limited is a Nigerian engineering services company. The company is developing Reliability Desk, an AI-assisted maintenance planning product concept for industrial plant and facilities teams.

Product status: A local-first browser prototype supports editable assets, maintenance events, CSV import and JSON backups. Six seeded records are fictional; scores use a transparent sample rule. Production AI, shared customer workspaces, verified pilots and cloud deployment are not yet available.

Company

Queen SusaStrategy Limited

Company materials describe engineering maintenance, mechanical installation, fabrication, shutdown support and construction services. The product initiative applies that industrial context to a focused maintenance-planning workflow. Company registration details are not published on this page.

Product direction

Reliability Desk

The planned product will help teams organize work orders, inspection notes and equipment history, then surface recurring signals for human review. Natural-language processing, anomaly detection and time-series analysis are being evaluated; model selection and benefits require validation with operators.

Who it is for

Industrial operators in Nigeria

The initial target users are plant maintenance managers, reliability engineers and operations leads responsible for equipment uptime. Market sizing and pilot results will be published only when independently substantiated.

Infrastructure direction

Planned cloud and GPU evaluation

AWS and Microsoft Azure are candidate platforms for storage, APIs, managed asset data, identity, monitoring and future AI inference. Options include Amazon S3, RDS, ECS or Lambda, Bedrock and CloudWatch, alongside Azure Blob Storage, Azure Database for PostgreSQL, Container Apps or Functions, Entra ID, Azure Monitor and Azure AI Foundry. The architecture and primary cloud provider have not been selected; a multi-cloud deployment is not claimed. NVIDIA CUDA, TensorRT and Triton may support model experimentation and inference optimization if measured workloads justify GPU use. These services are planned candidates, not currently deployed infrastructure.
