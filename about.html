AI business insights · MVP stage

Building a clearer way for small businesses to use data.

Queen SusaStrategy Limited is a Nigerian company building an AI-powered business-insights MVP for small-business owners and managers. The product focuses on helping users review authorized sales, customer-feedback and operations data, surface useful patterns and make informed decisions with human oversight.

Product status: Interactive browser prototype. It includes a local linear-regression revenue trend model and deterministic financial signals. It is not a hosted, multi-customer application; supported workflows, pilot activity and measured results should be confirmed with the team.

Product

Business and financial intelligence

The prototype covers financial performance, revenue trends, cash-flow planning, business recommendations, workforce-cost signals and business reports.

Responsible ML

Decision support with human review

A transparent linear-regression model estimates a revenue trend from user-entered history. The recommendations use deterministic rules; users should check all assumptions and context before acting.
